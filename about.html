About StudentHub

StudentHub is a centralized student portal designed to provide students with easy access to academic information and university activities.

Our Purpose

The purpose of StudentHub is to make student information easier to access, organize and manage.

Key Features

Academic Information

Students can view attendance and academic results.

Events

Students can discover upcoming university events and activities.

Student Profile

Students can view their personal and academic information.
