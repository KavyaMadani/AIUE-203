Frequently Asked Questions

What is StudentHub?

StudentHub is a student portal that provides access to academic information and university activities.

How can I register?

Visit the Register page and complete the registration form.

How can I log in?

Visit the Login page and enter your enrollment number and password.

Where can I check my attendance?

Open the Dashboard and select the Attendance option.

Where can I see university events?

Visit the Events page to see upcoming activities.

How can I contact StudentHub support?

Visit the Contact page and send us a message.
